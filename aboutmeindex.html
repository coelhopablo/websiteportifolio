Howdy folks! I'm Pablo Coelho!

For the past 10 years +, I've been knee-deep in paints, pixels, and everything in between. From slinging brushes to crafting animations, I've done it all. You can see some of my work on Rede Globo, Brazil's top TV channel. I've contributed to shows like "Programa Domingão do Faustão," "O Outro Lado do Paraíso," "Deus Salve o Rei," and others. With a Bachelor of Fine Arts from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro and a Master of Arts in Animation from Universidade Lusófona of Lisbon, I've got the credentials to back up my wild imagination. In my journey through these two places, I've leveled up all my skills in all fields of fine arts, digital arts, and web design. I've created Comics Hut, an Instagram and TikTok page to showcase all my comic creations. If you want to catch up, just follow @comicshut. There you'll find "The Moonyman" making its debut, and there's a whole universe of characters, stories, and surprises waiting to unfold. So, if you're up for a bit of creative chaos or just fancy a chat about life, art, or why beer is way better than red wine, hit me up on my social media channels.
